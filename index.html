Assumptions, formulas and limitations

Weekly recovered hours = people × eligible workflow hours/person/week × coverage ÷ 100. Monthly hours = weekly hours × 52 ÷ 12. Coverage is applied once; eligible hours must be entered before automation.

Monthly gross labor value = monthly recovered hours × hourly cost. Monthly net capacity value = monthly gross value − ongoing monthly costs. Annual gross value = weekly recovered hours × 52 × hourly cost.

Total modeled year-one cost = upfront implementation + 12 × monthly ongoing costs. Year-one net capacity value = annual gross value − total modeled year-one cost. Year-one ROI = net capacity value ÷ total modeled year-one cost × 100; it is undefined when modeled cost is zero.

Simple payback = upfront implementation ÷ positive net monthly capacity value. With positive net value and no upfront cost, payback is immediate; with nonpositive net monthly value there is no modeled payback. Values beyond 12 months are outside year one. Very short positive paybacks display as <0.1 months, not zero.

This is a simplified, undiscounted full-run-rate model, not a complete financial forecast. It excludes ramp-up, implementation delays, taxes, financing, discounting, cash realization and any costs you do not enter. Include all relevant ongoing costs. Values are rounded for display only; calculations use unrounded numbers. All amounts are USD; there is no FX conversion.
